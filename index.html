OFFICIAL HARBOR REGULATIONS

Water Race Rules

+2.0s

Countdown & Jump Start

Hold your hull behind the floating checkered start line during the 3-2-1 horn countdown. Crossing early before GO! adds a +2.0s jump-start penalty.

OUTSIDE

Buoy Rounding

Round every numbered apex turn buoy in sequence on its outside (between the apex buoy and the outer gate spar) following the glowing directional arrow.

+3.0s

Missed Buoy & Course Cut

Pass inside a turn buoy and you'll get a warning to loop back and round it cleanly. Skipping ahead to the next buoy incurs a +3.0s course-cut penalty.

+1.5s

Buoy Collision ("Buoy Whack")

Clipping a floating apex or gate buoy knocks it aside and adds a +1.5s time penalty. Give tight turns a little breathing room!

WAKE

Wake Turbulence

Crossing another boat's foaming V-wake creates choppy hull bounce and minor drag. Carve clean water to carry maximum planing speed.

3 LAPS

Adjusted Time Scoring

Complete 3 full laps. Final standings are ranked by Finish Time + Penalties. Finish 1st overall to add a Win to your career record!
